Our Story

A Journey of Growth

How we scaled from a small local agency to an international digital powerhouse over the last decade.

2017

The Foundation

Leo Digital Marketing was founded with a small team of 3 in a tiny co-working space, focusing purely on local SEO and basic social media management for local retail clients.

2019

Expanding the Arsenal

Introduced fully-fledged PPC and Content Marketing divisions. We hit our first milestone of 50 active clients and outgrew our original office space.

2021

Going International

Broke into the international market, acquiring major SaaS and E-commerce clients in Europe and North America. Team size scaled past 40 dedicated growth experts.

2023

Data-Driven Innovations

Launched our proprietary analytics framework, offering clients unprecedented transparency into ROI, customer acquisition costs, and multi-touch attribution modeling.

2026

The Future of Growth

Recognized as an industry leader with over 500+ successful brand campaigns. Continuing to innovate with AI-driven ad optimization and predictive SEO strategies.
